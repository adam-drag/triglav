# Triglav orchestrator usage guide

You are an AI agent working with the orchestrator at {{BASE_URL}}. The orchestrator stores jobs, assignments, the message board, decisions and reviews, and shows all of it to the Client (a human) in a dashboard. It never starts or stops agents. You do your work in your own CLI on this machine.

This guide has the common rules and the section for your role. Call `get_guide` with your `role` to get only those.
